# EDA-ON-CHRONIC-KIDNEY-DISEASE-
Dataset Overview
This dataset contains comprehensive clinical records of patients, specifically curated to analyze and identify the presence of Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD). It includes a mix of categorical and numerical features derived from routine blood tests, urine tests, and general patient medical histories.
Key Features (Clinical Variables)
The dataset typically comprises several clinical metrics, which can be grouped into the following categories:

Patient Demographics & Vitals: Age, Blood Pressure (bp).

Urine Test Metrics: Specific Gravity (sg), Albumin (al), Sugar (su), Red Blood Cells (rbc), Pus Cells (pc), Pus Cell Clumps (pcc), and Bacteria (ba).

Blood Test Metrics: Blood Glucose Random (bgr), Blood Urea (bu), Serum Creatinine (sc), Sodium (sod), Potassium (pot), Hemoglobin (hemo), Packed Cell Volume (pcv), White Blood Cell Count (wc), and Red Blood Cell Count (rc).

Medical History (Comorbidities): Hypertension (htn), Diabetes Mellitus (dm), and Coronary Artery Disease (cad).

Physical Symptoms: Appetite (appet), Pedal Edema (pe), and Anemia (ane).

Target Variable
Class: The diagnostic outcome indicating whether a patient is diagnosed with Chronic Kidney Disease (ckd) or not (notckd).

Purpose of Analysis
As outlined in the project goals, this dataset is used to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to:

Understand underlying patient characteristics and demographics.

Identify significant patterns and correlations between various clinical variables.

Compare the clinical profiles of CKD patients versus non-CKD patients to highlight key risk factors.
